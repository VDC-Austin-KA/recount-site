Three ways in, because one is never enough

Most dictation tools send synthetic keystrokes and stop there. That silently fails in remote sessions, which forward real key codes and have nothing to forward.

Typing

The default. Leaves your clipboard untouched and works in ordinary applications.

Pasting

For Teams, Java and Qt applications that ignore synthetic keystrokes. Your clipboard is put back afterwards.

Key presses

Real keystrokes matched to the target's keyboard layout — for Citrix and locked-down remote desktops where the clipboard is disabled by policy.

All three are built and switchable from the tray. The application names above are written in future tense on purpose: they have not yet been tested inside a real Citrix or remote desktop session, and until they are, that claim is not one to make.
